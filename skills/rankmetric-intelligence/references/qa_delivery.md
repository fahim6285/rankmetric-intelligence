# Quality gates and presentation

Check raw-to-final counts, independently calculate one sample metric, inspect duplicate exceptions, valid range of statuses, types, years, all 17 SDG bins where applicable, numerical precision and period alignment. Negative counts and corrupted IDs require review. Cross-check sum of disjoint groups equals total; do not expect overlapping groups to sum to unique works.

For spreadsheets: concise sheet names, freeze headers, practical column widths, explicit units, table titles, separate summary + source detail + discrepancies + source log and formulas for derived indicators. Keep straightforward, staff-readable designs; avoid needlessly nested pivots or unsupported score formulas. For documents: tables captioned consistently, clear audit summary and evidence references.

Never write generative instructions inside final institutional reports. Do not include a particular organization's name, confidential data, people or prior internal results in reusable skill or templates. Require user authorization for external publication/submission. Report completed tasks, actual counts and unresolved issues precisely.
