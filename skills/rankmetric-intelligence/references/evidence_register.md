# Traceable evidence controls

An evidence row minimally captures `framework,edition,indicator,period,evidence_id,document_name,owner,source_uri_or_path,source_page_or_row,collection_date,verification_date,status,exception,action_owner,due_date`.

Statuses: Verified (primary source inspected), Received (exists, not verified), Pending, Missing, Superseded, Not Applicable. Never mark verified merely because a filename exists. Records missing issue dates, authentication, signatures or table-to-source links remain pending review.

Check every cited exhibit, appendix, survey field and uploaded form against source. Record contradictions separately and explain sign-off conditions. Preserve source history; avoid rewriting an official certificate or filling missing evidence. Reconcile cross-department data with distinct record-level keys when possible.
