# Official methodology and edition-control protocol

For each ranking, accreditation or survey framework, record exact scheme name, issuing authority, edition/year, publication date, official document URI, section/page/table, effective period, and access date. Retrieve first-party methodology/rules prior to applying eligibility, numerator/denominator definitions, weightings, disciplinary normalization, self-reported versus externally extracted inputs, deadlines or scoring caps.

Never extrapolate older methods to current cycles or assume a number is official from a press summary. Official rules may be revised, replaced or subject to corrections. Different frameworks may share terms but assign different meanings. Make a rule ledger containing: `framework,edition,indicator,definition,formula,unit,period,source_url,section,verified_on,status`.

If the official version is unavailable: (1) present an evidence inventory or descriptive analysis not dependent on the formula; (2) label any formula as user-defined or provisional; (3) identify exact unresolved rule checks. Do not invent latest framework scores or comparisons.
