# Indicator dictionary and units

Maintain `indicator_id,framework,edition,period_convention,unit,entity_scope,numerator,denominator,aggregation_rule,missingness,rounding,source_of_truth,verification_status`. Explicitly distinguish research output per author, unique document volume, fractional counts, weighted citation impact, patents filed vs granted, funding sanctioned vs received, and staff FTE vs headcount.

Include reporting windows (e.g., calendar vs academic year), zero-denominator behavior, staff affiliation eligibility, multi-affiliation publications, fractional allocation, discipline/category mappings and units of currency. Do not infer institution-wide totals by adding overlapping school or SDG totals. Ratios and rates require a verified denominator, not a convenient proxy.

If frameworks request other outcomes (teaching, placement, graduation, resources, perception), verify their definitions separately. Do not substitute a generic metric for an official indicator.
